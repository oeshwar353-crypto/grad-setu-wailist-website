📖 Google Sheets Real-Time Sync Setup Guide

Follow these 3 simple steps to connect your GradSetu waitlist directly to Google Sheets:

🛡️ 100% Private to You (Zero Leakage)

Your Google Sheet itself remains completely private to your Google account (Restricted - Only you). The Web App acts strictly as an append-only API gateway with client-secret token verification.

1

Open Google Sheets & Apps Script

Create a fresh Google Sheet at sheets.new. Go to Extensions → Apps Script.

2

Copy & Paste google-apps-script/Code.gs

Delete any existing code in the editor, and paste the contents of your local file:

c:\...\GradSetu Waitlist LandingPage\google-apps-script\Code.gs
3

Deploy as Web App (Public Access)

Click Deploy → New deployment. Select type Web app:

  • Execute as: Me (your Google account)
  • Who has access: Anyone (Critical so students can register)

Click Deploy, authorize permissions, and copy the generated Web App URL.

💡 Connect with 1 Click

Click "Sheets Config" in the top bar and paste your URL. The app will immediately start writing every new pass directly into your Google Sheet!
